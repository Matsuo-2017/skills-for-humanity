## Key Question 1: Which open-weight models are closest to frontier proprietary models on reasoning, and by how much do they trail?

### Takeaway
As of September 2026 the open-weight frontier is held by Chinese labs — Zhipu/Z.ai GLM-5.3 (753B MoE), Moonshot Kimi K3 (2.8T MoE), Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6-Pro (>1T MoE), Alibaba Qwen3.8-Max (2.4T MoE) and DeepSeek V4-Pro (1.6T MoE) — with NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra (550B) the strongest US entrant. Independent trackers put the gap to the best proprietary model at roughly 4 months / ~8 Epoch Capabilities Index points (Epoch AI, measured since Jan 2026) and at 4–9 points on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (4 points at Kimi K3's July release, widening to ~9 after GPT-6 Astra shipped in early September 2026); on individual reasoning evals (GPQA Diamond, AIME 2025, SWE-bench Verified) the best open models are within 1–4 points of or tied with frontier closed models, while on Humanity's Last Exam they trail by ~2–10 points.

### Inferences
- Measured by aggregate indices, the open-closed gap in 2026 has oscillated between ~4 and ~9 points on AA's scale depending on when the latest closed model shipped; the Epoch "4 months" figure is the most stable single number to cite.
- On verifiable-answer reasoning evals (GPQA Diamond, AIME 2025, SWE-bench Verified) top open models are effectively at parity (within 1–4 pts); the residual gap concentrates in broad-knowledge/agentic evals (HLE, Terminal-Bench, GDPval-style tasks).
- The open frontier is now dominated by very large sparse MoEs (1T–2.8T total, 49B–104B active) from Chinese labs; US open releases (Nemotron 3 Ultra, Gemma 4, Muse Glimmer, gpt-oss) sit one tier lower on the indices.
- DeepSeek's R-series has effectively been folded into the V4 line (reasoning mode in V4-Pro-Max); "DeepSeek R2" should be treated as non-existent as of Sept 2026.

## Key Question 2: What are the canonical open-source repos for training a reasoning model (RL with verifiable rewards), and which are actively maintained?

### Takeaway
The RLVR stack has consolidated around six actively maintained trainers — verl (ByteDance/volcengine, 23.7k stars), OpenRLHF (10k), TRL (Hugging Face, 19.4k), Unsloth (77k, single-GPU focus), prime-rl (Prime Intellect, 2.1k, async FSDP2+vLLM) and SkyRL (NovaSky/Berkeley, 2.4k) — plus the SGLang-side slime/Miles pair (8.6k/3.0k) that gave Kimi K3 day-0 RL, and NVIDIA NeMo-RL (2.0k). All were pushed within the last two weeks (most on 2026-09-29) and all are Apache-2.0 except verifiers (MIT). Algorithmically, GRPO and its stabilized variants (DAPO, Dr.GRPO, GSPO, CISPO, REINFORCE++) are the standard recipes; DAPO's original repo and the OpenThoughts data repo are effectively frozen (last push May and Dec 2025 respectively).

## Key Question 3: What is the smallest model size that achieves credible multi-step reasoning, and what runs on a single consumer GPU or laptop?

### Takeaway
Credible multi-step reasoning now appears at 3–4B parameters: Weibo's VibeThinker-3B (June 2026, ~6 GB BF16) reports 94.3 on AIME 2026 (contested), and Qwen3.5-4B scores 76.2 on GPQA Diamond; the 9B–30B tier (Qwen3.5-9B: GPQA 81.7, 5.5 GB at Q4; gpt-oss-20b in 16 GB; Nemotron 3 Nano AIME25 89; Qwen3.6-27B in ~17 GB; Gemma 4 31B and Muse Glimmer 30B on a 24 GB card) is the practical laptop/single-consumer-GPU frontier, while frontier-class open MoEs (DeepSeek V4-Flash 284B, Kimi K3 2.8T) need 1–2 datacenter GPUs or SGLang's SSD-backed expert offload.
